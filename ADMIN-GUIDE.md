# Admin Guide — The Pink Haired Potter

**Where:** `https://yourdomain.com/admin` works on a phone or a computer. Bookmark it or add it to your phone's home screen.

**Logging in:** Enter `thepinkhairedpotter@gmail.com`. Cloudflare emails a 6-digit code; type it in and you stay logged in for about a month.

Everything you change here goes live right away, with no code and no AI needed.

---

## Add a new piece to the shop
1. **Shop items → + New item**.
2. **Choose files** to add photos straight from your phone camera roll. The first photo is the main one; use ◀ ▶ to reorder and ✕ to remove.
3. Fill in:
   - **Title**, e.g. "Pink Party Mug".
   - **Price**.
   - **Packed weight**: the weight of the piece *in its shipping box with padding*. This sets the shipping cost.
   - **Quantity**: usually 1 for one-of-a-kind pieces.
   - **Category**, e.g. Mugs, Cups, Bowls, or Dinnerware. The shop shows filter buttons when there are 2 or more categories.
   - **Description**: size, capacity, colors, and care notes. Leave a blank line between paragraphs.
4. Check **Feature on the home page** to show it in "Fresh from the kiln."
5. **Save**. It's live.

Photos are shrunk automatically before uploading, so full-size phone photos are fine.

## When something sells
- **Online sales** take care of themselves: the piece disappears from the shop and the order appears in **Orders**.
- **Sold in person** (craft fair, friend): open the piece → **Mark sold**.
- **Want to show it off after it sells?** Open the piece → **Copy photos to gallery**.
- To take something off the shop temporarily, check **Hide from the shop**.

The **Sold** filter shows past pieces. Open one and set the quantity back above 0 to sell it again.

## Orders
Each order shows the customer, items, shipping address (or **Pickup**), and amounts.
- Set the **Status** (packed → shipped / picked up) and put the tracking number in **Notes**.
- A **"ZIP differs from estimate"** tag means the shipping address ZIP differs from the ZIP used for the estimate. Double-check postage before buying the label.
- Refunds are done in Stripe (the **Open Stripe** button).

## Custom requests
Requests from the Custom Orders form land here, along with any reference photo the customer uploaded.
1. Reply to the customer by email to work out the design and price.
2. To collect a deposit or payment:
   - Enter the **Amount** and a **Label**, e.g. "Teal mug set – 50% deposit".
   - Click **Create payment link**. The link is copied automatically; paste it into your email.
   - The link works **once**. When they pay, the request is marked **Deposit paid** automatically.
3. Track progress with **Status** (quoted → deposit paid → in progress → complete) and private **Notes**.

## Gallery
- **Add photos:** choose one or more photos, optionally type a **Collection** (e.g. Mugs) and a caption, then click **Upload to gallery**.
- **Edit:** change the caption or collection, then click **Save**.
- Use **← →** to change the order and 🗑 to delete.
- The gallery shows filter buttons when there are 2 or more collections.

## Page text
Edit the banner, home page text, artist statement, custom order intro, care instructions, policies, pickup address, contact email, and social links. Click **Save all text**.
- **Announcement bar:** type something like "Holiday market Dec 6 – come say hi!" and it appears at the top of every page. Clear it to hide the bar.
- The **pickup address** is private. Only customers who paid for a pickup order see it.

## Shipping
- Set prices by total **packed** weight, e.g. up to 3 lbs = $12, up to 6 lbs = $18.
- Optional settings:
  - **Free shipping** over a dollar amount.
  - A **far-away ZIP surcharge**, e.g. +$5 for West Coast ZIPs starting with 9.
  - **Local pickup** on or off.
- Use **Try it** to preview the charge, then **Save shipping**.
- Tip: get a few real postage quotes (USPS, UPS, or a discount site like Pirate Ship) for a boxed mug and a boxed set, and set the tiers slightly above them to cover boxes and padding.

## Photo tips
- Shoot in soft, natural light (near a window, no direct sun) on a plain background.
- Take several angles, plus a close-up of the glitter resin and one with a hand or object for scale.
- Square photos look best. The shop crops to a square.
